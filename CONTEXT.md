# Paremia Context

## What It Is
A hand-curated corpus of cross-cultural proverbs grouped into concept clusters.
Every proverb carries notes and every concept carries an attestation_source.

## Why It Exists
To surface universal human patterns — hypocrisy, patience, greed, etc. —
through the lens of how every culture expresses them.
Paremia is the fifth venture in Niko's lab portfolio.

## Corpus Versions
- v0.1.0: initial release
- v0.2.0: 683 proverbs, 67 clusters, 29 languages (current)

## Key Files
- proverbs.json — the full corpus (do not edit by hand)
- validate.py — schema + counts self-checker
- art/ — 67 engraved concept plates (FLUX.2 Klein)
- about.html, contribute.html, index.html, app.js, style.css — MVP site
